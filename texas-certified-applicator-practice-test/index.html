How the Texas exams work

Structural pest control in Texas is licensed by the Texas Department of Agriculture's Structural Pest Control Service under Occupations Code Chapter 1951 and the SPCS rules in 4 TAC Chapter 7. Before you can sit for an exam you must be pre-qualified by TDA: submit your application and license fees, receive your Examination Eligibility Letter, then register and schedule with Metro Institute, which has administered the exams at 30+ Texas sites since May 2025. There is a separate exam for each category, the exam fee is $64 per category, and a minimum grade of 70% earns the license in that category. Scores stay valid for 12 months.
