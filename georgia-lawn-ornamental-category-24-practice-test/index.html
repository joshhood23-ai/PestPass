Lawn & Ornamental

Which fertility practice is correct for centipedegrass?

  1. A. Target soil pH 5.0–6.0, apply phosphorus only when a soil test calls for it, and do not overseed ryegrass into it
  2. B. Target soil pH 6.5–7.0 and apply phosphorus routinely every spring
  3. C. Overseed ryegrass each fall to protect the centipede crowns from cold
  4. D. Apply nitrogen at bermudagrass rates of 4–5 pounds per 1,000 square feet yearly, regardless of weather conditions
Correct answer: A. Target soil pH 5.0–6.0, apply phosphorus only when a soil test calls for it, and do not overseed ryegrass into it
Centipedegrass fertility cautions: target soil pH 5.0–6.0, skip phosphorus unless a soil test calls for it, and do NOT overseed ryegrass on centipedegrass.

How are chinch bugs sampled in turf?

  1. A. Cutting a 1-square-foot flap of turf and folding it back to count insects in the soil
  2. B. Pouring a soap solution over the turf to drive insects to the surface
  3. C. Under all conditions, sweeping a net across the turf surface at dusk to catch feeding insects, no matter the circumstances
  4. D. Pushing an open-ended can 2–3 inches into the turf, filling it with water, and counting the bugs that float to the surface
Correct answer: D. Pushing an open-ended can 2–3 inches into the turf, filling it with water, and counting the bugs that float to the surface
Chinch bugs are sampled by flotation: an open-ended can is pushed 2–3 inches into the turf, filled with water, and the bugs that float to the surface are counted.

An insect 1/8 to 1/4 inch long with a lacy, transparent hood and wings is feeding on azalea leaves. What is it?

  1. A. A lace bug
  2. B. A young whitefly
  3. C. A thrips adult
  4. D. A spider mite
Correct answer: A. A lace bug
Lace bugs are 1/8 to 1/4 inch long with a lacy, transparent hood and wings — the ornate, lace-like appearance gives them their name.

Which turfgrass is most seriously injured by the southern chinch bug in Georgia?

  1. A. Tall fescue
  2. B. Kentucky bluegrass
  3. C. St. Augustinegrass
  4. D. Perennial ryegrass
Correct answer: C. St. Augustinegrass
St. Augustinegrass is the turf most seriously injured by the southern chinch bug, although zoysiagrass, bermudagrass, bahiagrass, and centipedegrass are also attacked.

How can centipedegrass be distinguished from bermudagrass in the field?

  1. A. Centipedegrass has broad blades at least one-quarter inch wide with boat-shaped tips
  2. B. Centipedegrass spreads by both rhizomes and stolons, while bermudagrass uses stolons only
  3. C. Centipedegrass blades fold along a strong center vein, it has a 'Granny Smith apple' green color, and it spreads by stolons only — no rhizomes
  4. D. Centipedegrass has short, sharp blades and a much darker blue-green color, regardless of the weather, the site conditions, or the time of year
Correct answer: C. Centipedegrass blades fold along a strong center vein, it has a 'Granny Smith apple' green color, and it spreads by stolons only — no rhizomes
Centipedegrass blades are wider than bermudagrass (still under 1/4 inch) and fold along a strong center vein; healthy centipede is a distinctive 'Granny Smith apple' green and spreads by stolons ONLY — it has no rhizomes.

What minimum liability insurance must a Georgia GROUND pesticide contractor carry?

  1. A. Bodily injury $50,000 and property damage $25,000 per occurrence
  2. B. Bodily injury $25,000 and property damage $10,000 per occurrence
  3. C. Bodily injury $100,000 and property damage $100,000 per occurrence
  4. D. Bodily injury $10,000 and property damage $25,000 per occurrence
Correct answer: B. Bodily injury $25,000 and property damage $10,000 per occurrence
Ground contractor minimums are bodily injury $25,000 and property damage $10,000 for any one occurrence (Rule 40-21-8-.03). The $50,000/$25,000 figures are the higher aerial-contractor minimums.

Which of these landscape treatments is exempt from Georgia's posting rule?

  1. A. A foliar spray to shrubs along a public sidewalk
  2. B. A granular fertilizer-herbicide spread on a front lawn
  3. C. A direct injection into the trunk of a landscape tree
  4. D. A broadcast spray to turf at an office park entrance
Correct answer: C. A direct injection into the trunk of a landscape tree
Posting exemptions cover direct plant injections, a homeowner treating their own single-family residence, and interior plantscapes treated at least 2 hours before the area is open to public access.

A bermudagrass lawn suffers mild-to-moderate fall armyworm feeding, but the stolons are intact. What is the expected outcome?

  1. A. The lawn will always need resodding after any armyworm feeding
  2. B. Recovery takes a full year regardless of how light the damage is
  3. C. Healthy turf usually recovers in 3–4 weeks with fertility and irrigation
  4. D. Fertility and irrigation slow recovery and should be withheld, in all cases
Correct answer: C. Healthy turf usually recovers in 3–4 weeks with fertility and irrigation
Healthy turf usually recovers from mild-to-moderate fall armyworm feeding in 3–4 weeks with fertility and irrigation. Resodding is needed only if the stolons themselves were eaten.

Why is tea scale considered one of the harder scales to bring under control?

  1. A. Its armor is completely waterproof to all sprays
  2. B. It has a single generation that is easy to miss, regardless of conditions
  3. C. It has multiple overlapping generations and reproduces nearly year-round
  4. D. It spends most of its life hidden inside the stems
Correct answer: C. It has multiple overlapping generations and reproduces nearly year-round
Tea scale has multiple overlapping generations and reproduces nearly continuously through the year, so some life stage is almost always protected — which makes it hard to control.

Common questions

Is Georgia lawn & ornamental Category 21 or 24?

Category 24. Category 24 is Georgia's Commercial Pesticide Applicator category for ornamental and turf pest control.

How long is a Georgia Category 24 license good for?

Five years. The license fee is $90, renewal is $25, and recertification requires 10 hours of approved training per 5-year period. Application records must be kept for 2 years.

What score passes the Category 24 exam?

70%, the standard Georgia applicator pass mark. PestPass grades Category 24 practice at the same mark.
