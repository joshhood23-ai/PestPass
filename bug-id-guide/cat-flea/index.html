Cat Flea

Ctenocephalides felis — despite the name, the flea of both cats and dogs. Only about 1/8 inch, flattened side-to-side with powerful jumping hind legs and combs on the head. Most of an infestation is not on the pet at all — it is eggs, larvae and pupae in the carpet.

Flattened side-to-sideA thin, vertical body built for moving through fur
Head combsGenal and pronotal combs — the microscope marks
Jumping hind legsThe longest jumpers for their size in the house

How to identify it

  • Size first: about 1/8 inch — small, dark reddish-brown, and hard to hold still long enough to look at.
  • Side-to-side flat: the body is compressed laterally (thin when viewed from above, tall from the side) — the opposite of a bed bug’s top-to-bottom flat oval.
  • The combs: rows of spines (genal comb on the cheek, pronotal comb behind the head) visible under magnification — the marks that key it out from other fleas.
  • Hind legs: long and powerful; a flea that vanishes in a jump when touched is behaving exactly like a flea.
  • Where you find it: on pets around the neck, back and tail base — and its "flea dirt" (digested-blood droppings) in the pet’s bedding and resting spots.

It never feeds on blood, has no hardened shell, and its small size and soft body separate it from bed bug nymphs and fleas.

Signs you have them

  • "Flea dirt" — black pepper-like specks in pet bedding and carpet that turn reddish-brown on a wet paper towel (it is digested blood).
  • Pets scratching, biting at the tail base, or losing hair along the back.
  • Bites around human ankles after time in an infested room — especially in a home that sat empty, when starved adults emerge from cocoons all at once.
  • Eggs and larvae in carpet pile and upholstery where pets rest — the off-host 95% of the population.

General Knowledge

Which insects undergo COMPLETE metamorphosis?

  1. Grasshoppers, crickets, and earwigs
  2. Silverfish, firebrats, and lice
  3. Ants, beetles, flies, and fleas
  4. Cockroaches, termites, and bed bugs
Answer: Ants, beetles, flies, and fleas. Complete metamorphosis (egg, larva, pupa, adult) includes ants, bees, wasps, all beetles, flies, moths, and fleas. The other options list insects with incomplete metamorphosis (egg, nymph, adult — cockroaches, termites, grasshoppers, bed bugs, lice) or no metamorphosis (silverfish, firebrats).
General Knowledge

Approximately what percentage of a flea infestation are adults on the host?

  1. Only about 5% of the total flea population
  2. About 50% of the total flea population, mostly adults
  3. About 25% of the flea population
  4. About 75% of the flea population
Answer: Only about 5% of the total flea population. Only about 5% of fleas are adult fleas on the host. The remaining 95% — eggs, larvae, and pupae — are in the environment where the host rests.
HPC

Which flea species causes most infestations?

  1. Human flea (Pulex irritans)
  2. Dog flea (Ctenocephalides canis)
  3. Rat flea (Xenopsylla cheopis)
  4. Cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis)
Answer: Cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis). The cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis) causes the vast majority of flea infestations — it feeds on cats, dogs, wildlife, and humans.

Cat Flea questions, answered

Cat flea or dog flea?

Under a microscope they are near-twins: both carry the same genal and pronotal combs, and the dog flea’s head is slightly more rounded. In practice the cat flea infests both cats and dogs and is the species behind nearly every household flea problem — host and combs together point to cat flea.

Why do fleas keep coming back after treatment?

The pupae. Flea cocoons in carpet and upholstery are nearly impervious to insecticides and can wait months, emerging when vibration and warmth signal a host. Adults on the pet are only about 5% of the population — the eggs, larvae and pupae in the environment are the rest, which is why on-pet treatment plus vacuuming must run for weeks.

Flea or bed bug?

Fleas are laterally flattened (thin side-to-side), have long jumping hind legs, and live on pets and in their bedding. Bed bugs are flat top-to-bottom, oval like an apple seed, cannot jump, and hide in mattress seams and furniture. Behavior separates them before anatomy does.

Do cat fleas bite people?

Yes — especially around the ankles, and especially when the pet is removed or the home has been empty. People are a secondary host, but a heavy indoor population will feed on whoever is available.
