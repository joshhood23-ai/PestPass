Black Widow

Latrodectus mactans — the southern black widow. The female’s body is about 3/8 inch (leg span to about 1 1/2 inches), glossy black with a round abdomen, and the famous mark — a red hourglass on the underside of the abdomen.

How to identify it

  • Size first: female body about 3/8 inch with a leg span up to about 1 1/2 inches; males are much smaller, slimmer and rarely noticed.
  • The hourglass: two red triangles meeting at a point, on the underside of the round abdomen. Flip the spider’s posture, not the spider — view from below or from the side profile of a hanging female.
  • Glossy black all over — legs and body — with a bulbous, almost spherical abdomen. Brown widows are mottled tan; false widows lack the vivid hourglass.
  • The web: a low, tangled, irregular cobweb with a retreat at the edge, built in undisturbed places close to the ground.
  • Where you find it: woodpiles, meter and valve boxes, under stones and steps, in garage corners and crawlspaces — sheltered, quiet, rarely trafficked spots.

Signs you have them

  • Low, messy cobwebs in undisturbed corners at ground level, with a glossy black spider hanging beneath.
  • Egg sacs in the web — smooth, round, cream-colored balls about 3/8 inch across (brown widow sacs are spiky by contrast).
  • Webs in the same sheltered spots season after season: meter boxes, woodpiles, under porch steps.
  • Prey remains — beetles and other hard-bodied insects — collected under the web.

The two medically significant spiders in Georgia are:

  1. Black widow and brown recluse
  2. Wolf spider and jumping spider
  3. Banana spider and orb weaver
  4. Yellow sac spider and house spider
Answer: Black widow and brown recluse. Black widow (shiny black female, red hourglass on abdomen) and brown recluse (tan/brown, violin marking, 6 eyes in 3 pairs) are Georgia's two medically significant species.

Where is the red hourglass marking on a black widow?

  1. On the top of the abdomen
  2. On the chelicerae (fangs)
  3. On the top of the cephalothorax
  4. On the underside of the abdomen
Answer: On the underside of the abdomen. The red hourglass marking on a black widow is located on the underside (ventral surface) of the round, shiny black abdomen.

Which detail distinguishes a black widow spider from a similar-looking but harmless cobweb spider?

  1. A red or orange hourglass on the underside of a glossy black abdomen
  2. Black widows are incapable of producing any silk webbing
  3. Cobweb spiders are always noticeably larger in body size than black widows
  4. Black widows are found exclusively indoors, never outdoors
Answer: A red or orange hourglass on the underside of a glossy black abdomen. The red or orange hourglass marking on the underside of the abdomen, combined with the glossy black rounded body, is the identifying feature that separates black widows from otherwise similar-looking harmless cobweb spiders.

Black Widow questions, answered

How do I identify a black widow?

A glossy black female with a round, bulbous abdomen and a red hourglass marking on its underside, sitting in a low tangled web in an undisturbed spot. Body about 3/8 inch; leg span up to about 1 1/2 inches. No other common spider combines the gloss, the round abdomen and the hourglass.

Black widow or brown widow?

Color and egg sacs: black widows are uniformly glossy black with a vivid red hourglass, and their egg sacs are smooth. Brown widows are mottled tan-brown with a darker orange hourglass, and their egg sacs are covered in spikes, like tiny maces.

How dangerous is a black widow bite?

Widows are shy and bite mainly when trapped against skin (in a glove, shoe or woodpile). The venom is medically significant — muscle pain and cramping can develop over hours — so any suspected widow bite, especially for children, warrants prompt medical advice. That said, most encounters end with the spider retreating.

Where do black widows hide around a house?

Anywhere quiet and undisturbed near the ground: woodpiles, meter boxes, under steps and stones, garage and crawlspace corners, and stacked items. Wear gloves for exactly those jobs.
