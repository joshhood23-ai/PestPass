Brown-banded Cockroach

Supella longipalpa — the German cockroach’s same-size rival, separated by one mark: instead of stripes on the shield, it wears two light bands across the wings. Adults are about 1/2 inch. Unlike the German, it spreads through whole buildings, including dry rooms far from water.

How to identify it

  • Size first: about 1/2 inch — the same size class as the German cockroach, and the two are confused constantly.
  • The bands: two pale, transverse bands run across the wings and abdomen. The German’s marks are two dark stripes on the pronotum instead — check which body part carries the pattern.
  • Nymphs: small and wingless with two pale transverse bands crossing a darker brown abdomen (a German nymph shows a single pale band down the center of its back).
  • Where you find it: everywhere in a structure — bedrooms, closets, behind picture frames, inside electronics and furniture — because it needs less moisture than the German cockroach.
  • Egg cases: females glue their small oothecae to hidden surfaces — under furniture, in closets — instead of carrying them like the German cockroach does.

Signs you have them

  • Roaches in bedrooms, living rooms and offices — rooms with no plumbing — point to brown-banded rather than German.
  • Egg cases glued under furniture, inside drawers, or behind picture frames and wall hangings.
  • Roaches high on walls and ceilings; this species uses the whole room, not just floor-level harborage.
  • Activity inside electronics and appliances throughout the building, not only in the kitchen.

Brown-banded Cockroach questions, answered

How do I tell a brown-banded cockroach from a German cockroach?

Both are about 1/2 inch. The brown-banded has two light bands across its wings; the German has two dark stripes on the pronotum (the shield behind the head) and no wing bands. Location helps too: brown-bandeds spread into dry rooms, Germans stay near kitchen and bathroom moisture.

How big is a brown-banded cockroach?

About 1/2 inch as an adult — the same size as a German cockroach, and far smaller than the big outdoor species.

Where do brown-banded cockroaches hide?

Throughout a building: behind picture frames, in closets and drawers, under furniture, inside electronics. Females glue their egg cases to those hidden surfaces, so the cases turn up far from kitchens and bathrooms.

Do brown-banded cockroaches fly?

Males are capable fliers and will fly when disturbed, especially in warm rooms; females have shorter wings and mostly jump and run. A small roach flying at a light is often this species.
