German Cockroach

Blattella germanica — the most common cockroach in homes, apartments and restaurants. Adults are only 1/2 to 5/8 inch long, light brown, with two dark parallel stripes on the shield behind the head. That one mark settles the ID.

How to identify it

  • Size first: 1/2 to 5/8 inch. If the roach is well over an inch long, it is not a German cockroach — look at the big outdoor species below.
  • The two stripes: two dark lines run front to back on the pronotum, the shield behind the head. No other common house roach has them.
  • Color: light brown to tan, with wings covering the whole body. Nymphs are smaller and darker, with a tan stripe down the back.
  • Where you find it: indoors only, clustered near food and moisture — kitchens and bathrooms, under sinks, behind refrigerators, inside cabinet hinges. It does not live outdoors.
  • The egg case: the female carries her ootheca — a light-brown, purse-shaped case holding 30 to 40 eggs — protruding from her abdomen until just before it hatches.

Signs you have them

  • Live roaches scatter when a cabinet or appliance panel is opened — German cockroaches are almost never a single roach.
  • Finding several nymph sizes at once means an established, breeding population, not a stray.
  • Dark pepper-like droppings (frass) along cabinet hinges, drawer corners and under sinks.
  • Empty or carried oothecae in harborages — each case that hatched released 30 to 40 nymphs nearby.

Which statement about German cockroach reproductive capacity is most accurate?

  1. German cockroaches produce only a single solitary egg per ootheca
  2. Ootheca production stops permanently after a female’s first reproductive cycle
  3. German cockroaches cannot reproduce indoors under any conditions
  4. An ootheca holds 30-40 eggs, and females produce several in a lifetime
Answer: An ootheca holds 30-40 eggs, and females produce several in a lifetime. A German cockroach ootheca typically holds around 30-40 eggs, and a female can produce several oothecae over her lifetime — this reproductive output is a major reason German cockroach infestations can escalate so quickly if left unaddressed.

Which detail distinguishes the German cockroach ootheca-carrying behavior from that of the American cockroach?

  1. German cockroaches are believed to never actually produce an ootheca of any kind
  2. American cockroaches carry their ootheca for several months before finally dropping it
  3. German roaches carry the ootheca near hatching; American roaches drop theirs early
  4. Both species are believed to behave in an identical manner regarding ootheca care
Answer: German roaches carry the ootheca near hatching; American roaches drop theirs early. German cockroach females carry their egg case nearly to the point of hatching, offering it more protection, while American cockroaches typically deposit or drop their ootheca in a sheltered spot well before the eggs are ready to hatch.

German Cockroach questions, answered

How big is a German cockroach?

About 1/2 to 5/8 inch long as an adult — one of the smallest cockroaches found in homes and restaurants, and much smaller than the big outdoor species.

What are the two stripes on a German cockroach?

Two dark, parallel stripes run front to back on the pronotum, the shield-like plate behind the head. No other common house cockroach has them, so that one mark settles the ID.

German cockroach vs brown-banded cockroach — what's the difference?

Both are about 1/2 inch. The German has the two dark stripes on the pronotum; the brown-banded has light bands across its wings instead, and it spreads through whole buildings — including upper cabinets and behind picture frames — while German cockroaches stay close to kitchen and bathroom moisture.

Where do German cockroaches hide?

In tight harborages near food and water: under sinks, inside cabinet hinges, behind refrigerators and stoves, and around appliance motors. Females carry their egg case (ootheca) until just before it hatches, so one roach brought in with groceries or a used appliance imports a protected generation of 30 to 40 nymphs.
