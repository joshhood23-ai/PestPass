Silverfish

Lepisma saccharinum — a wingless primitive insect, about 1/2 inch, with a tapered, carrot-shaped silvery body and three long tail filaments. It grazes on paper, book glue and wallpaper paste in humid bathrooms, basements and attics.

How to identify it

  • Size first: about 1/2 inch of body, plus antennae and three tail filaments nearly as long again.
  • The shape: wide at the head, tapering steadily to the rear like a carrot or a teardrop, covered in silvery-gray scales that rub off like dust.
  • Three tails: three long, equal filaments trail from the rear (plus two long antennae up front — five "whiskers" in all). Earwigs, the other tail-bearing house insect, carry pincers instead.
  • Movement: a fast, wiggling, fish-like run when disturbed — the source of the name — usually at night.
  • Where you find it: humid, undisturbed places: bathrooms, basements, attics, behind wallpaper, in stored books and boxes of papers. Its mottled gray-brown cousin, the firebrat, prefers heat — near furnaces and ovens.

Signs you have them

  • Irregular, scraped feeding marks and yellowish stains on book pages, wallpaper and paper packaging.
  • Tiny black pepper-like droppings near books, papers and stored linens.
  • Shed skins and silvery scales left in sinks, tubs and storage boxes.
  • Live insects darting away when stored papers, books or boxes are disturbed at night.

Silverfish require which environmental condition to thrive?

  1. Dry air below 40% relative humidity
  2. Direct soil contact with their nesting material
  3. Bright light and high temperatures above 85°F
  4. High humidity, above 75% relative humidity
Answer: High humidity, above 75% relative humidity. Silverfish are moisture-dependent insects that require high relative humidity (>75% RH). They feed on starch — paper, book bindings, wallpaper paste, and sizing.
HPC

Which insect is most likely to indicate a chronic moisture problem within a structure?

  1. Brown recluse spider
  2. German cockroach
  3. The silverfish
  4. Pharaoh ant
Answer: The silverfish. Silverfish require high humidity to survive and are strongly associated with persistently damp areas — basements, crawlspaces, and water-damaged wall voids. Their presence indoors is a reliable indicator of a chronic moisture issue that should be investigated.
HPC

Which detail distinguishes silverfish and firebrats developmentally from insects that undergo complete or incomplete metamorphosis?

  1. They undergo complete metamorphosis, including a true, distinct pupal stage before adulthood
  2. They skip the egg stage entirely, developing directly from adult tissue instead
  3. They’re incapable of molting again after their very first juvenile molt
  4. They hatch as miniature adults with no larval or pupal stage, molting throughout adult life
Answer: They hatch as miniature adults with no larval or pupal stage, molting throughout adult life. Silverfish and firebrats are primitive, wingless insects that hatch looking like miniature adults and continue molting throughout their adult lives — a developmental pattern (ametabolous development) distinct from both complete and incomplete metamorphosis.

Silverfish questions, answered

How do I identify a silverfish?

A tapered, carrot-shaped silvery body about 1/2 inch long, long antennae, and three long tail filaments at the rear — moving with a wiggling, fish-like run. Nothing else common in houses combines the silver scales with the three tails.

Silverfish or firebrat?

Same shape, different dress and address: silverfish are uniformly silvery-gray and live in cool humidity (bathrooms, basements); firebrats are mottled gray-brown and live where it is hot — near furnaces, water heaters and ovens.

Do silverfish bite?

No. Silverfish have weak chewing mouthparts for grazing paper, glue and starches; they do not bite people or pets and spread no disease. They are a nuisance and a genuine threat to books, photos, wallpaper and stored papers.

What attracts silverfish?

Humidity and undisturbed carbohydrates: damp bathrooms and basements, stored books and cardboard, wallpaper paste. Reducing indoor humidity and removing paper clutter in damp rooms removes both halves of the attraction.
