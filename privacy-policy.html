PestPass · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026
⚠️ Test version: This app is currently in testing. Features, questions, and content may change before the final release.
The short version: PestPass doesn't collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app — quiz scores, flashcard progress, bookmarks, streaks, settings — stays stored locally on your own device. The one exception: so we can tell how many people use PestPass and how often, the app sends a single anonymous usage count each time it is opened (see "Anonymous usage counting" below). It contains no personal information and nothing about your studying. The only other time anything leaves your device is if you personally choose to email us feedback or a question report (see below), and you can read the message before it goes.

What this app is

PestPass (pest-pass.vercel.app) is a self-contained study tool for the Georgia Structural Pest Control Commission (SPCC) Certified Operator, Registered Technician, and DNR Nuisance Wildlife Control exam tracks. Your study data lives entirely on your device; the only things that ever leave it are the anonymous usage count described below, and anything you choose to email us.

Information we collect

No personal information. PestPass does not create user accounts, does not ask for your name, email address, or location, and never receives your study data. The app does not know who you are. Apart from the anonymous usage count below, we only receive information if you choose to email us (see "Feedback, question reports, and backups").

Anonymous usage counting

So we can tell how many people use PestPass and how often, the app sends one small count to our own server each time it is opened. Here is exactly what that involves:

  • The message contains a random identifier generated on your device, the app version, and nothing else. The identifier is a string of random characters — it is not your name, not your device's serial number or advertising ID, and it is never shared with anyone.
  • On our side, the identifier is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, and the counter keeps simple totals: how many times the app was opened each day, and how many different identifiers have opened it.
  • The counter does not store your IP address, and it never receives quiz answers, scores, categories, bookmarks, or any other study activity.
  • It uses no cookies and does not track you across other apps or websites. If you are offline when you open the app, nothing is sent.

Information stored on your device

To let you track progress and pick up where you left off, the app stores the following locally on your device only, using standard browser storage (localStorage):

  • Quiz scores, flashcard progress, and spaced-repetition scheduling data
  • Bookmarked questions and study streak history
  • App preferences (theme, selected exam track)
  • A short log of recent app error messages (up to 15 entries: the error text and the line of code it came from, with no study answers or personal details). It is used only to help diagnose problems and is never sent unless you tick "Include technical info" when sending feedback.
  • If you choose to enable it, an app-lock username and password — stored and checked entirely on-device; we never see them and they're never transmitted anywhere
  • The random anonymous identifier used for the usage count described above. It is generated on your device and is not linked to your identity; clearing the app's site data deletes it (a new one is generated the next time the app is opened)

With the single exception of the anonymous identifier (which is sent only as the usage count described above), none of this data is sent to us. We have no way to access your study data, back it up, or see it, because it is never transmitted to us.

Feedback, question reports, and backups

These features only act when you tap them, and nothing is sent in the background.

  • Send Feedback and "Report a problem": these open your own email app with a message already written to pestpassoffice@gmail.com. A question report includes the question's ID, category and text, the answer shown, the note you typed, the app and content version, and your exam track. If you tick "Include technical info," it also adds your device and browser type, screen size, whether the app is installed, and the recent error messages described above. You can preview and edit all of it before sending, or copy it instead of emailing. The message travels through your own email provider, and we will see your email address if you send it. We use reports only to fix and improve PestPass, and we'll delete a report on request.
  • Backup and Restore: "Save backup file" or "Copy backup text" creates a copy of your on-device study progress that you keep (for example in Files, email, or a cloud drive you choose). It is never uploaded to us. App-lock usernames and passwords are not included. Anyone who has your backup file can see your study progress, so store it somewhere you trust.

Analytics, ads, and third parties

PestPass contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no data is sold or shared with anyone. The only measurement is our own anonymous usage counter described above, which we run ourselves on our hosting platform.

Internet connection

PestPass works fully offline after the first load. It doesn't need an internet connection to function. The anonymous usage count is the only thing the app ever sends on its own, and only when a connection is available. (Sending a feedback email naturally requires your email app to be online.)

Children's privacy

PestPass is a professional exam-preparation tool intended for adults pursuing pest control or wildlife control licensure and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. The anonymous usage count contains no personal information.

Deleting your data

Since everything is stored locally, you're always in full control of it:

  • Use Settings → Reset Progress in the app to clear your study data at any time
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this app, or uninstalling the app, removes everything permanently, including the on-device error log and the anonymous usage identifier. The usage totals already counted contain no personal information and cannot be traced back to you or your device
  • Backup files you saved, and any emails you sent us, are outside the app. Delete your backup files yourself, and email us if you'd like a report you sent deleted

Changes to this policy

If anything about this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated first, with the "last updated" date above reflecting the change.
