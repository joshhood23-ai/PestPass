PestPass · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026
The short version: PestPass doesn't collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app — quiz scores, flashcard progress, bookmarks, streaks, settings — stays stored locally on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, because there's no server for it to go to.

What this app is

PestPass (pest-pass.vercel.app) is a self-contained study tool for the Georgia Structural Pest Control Commission (SPCC) Certified Operator, Registered Technician, and DNR Nuisance Wildlife Control exam tracks. It runs entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. PestPass does not have a server, does not create user accounts, and does not request or transmit any personal information. The app does not know who you are, where you are, or what device you're using.

Information stored on your device

To let you track progress and pick up where you left off, the app stores the following locally on your device only, using standard browser storage (localStorage):

  • Quiz scores, flashcard progress, and spaced-repetition scheduling data
  • Bookmarked questions and study streak history
  • App preferences (theme, selected exam track)
  • If you choose to enable it, an app-lock PIN — stored and checked entirely on-device; we never see it and it's never transmitted anywhere

None of this data ever leaves your device. We have no way to access it, back it up, or see it, because it's never sent to us in the first place.

Analytics, ads, and third parties

PestPass contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party services of any kind. No data is shared with anyone because no data is collected in the first place.

Internet connection

PestPass works fully offline after the first load. It doesn't need — and doesn't use — an internet connection to function.

Children's privacy

PestPass is a professional exam-preparation tool intended for adults pursuing pest control or wildlife control licensure and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we don't collect information at all.

Deleting your data

Since everything is stored locally, you're always in full control of it:

  • Use Settings → Reset Progress in the app to clear your study data at any time
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this app, or uninstalling the app, removes everything permanently

Changes to this policy

If anything about this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated first, with the "last updated" date above reflecting the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pestpassoffice@gmail.com.

PestPass is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Georgia Department of Agriculture, the Georgia Structural Pest Control Commission, or the Georgia Department of Natural Resources.
