Florida Regulations

Before applying for General Household Pest Control certification, an applicant must participate in a minimum number of jobs in Florida under the supervision of a certified operator in each category sought, documented on Form FDACS-13653. Under Rule 5E-14.117, that minimum is:

  1. A. 25 jobs in each category sought
  2. B. 30 jobs in each category sought
  3. C. 60 jobs in each category sought
  4. D. 45 jobs in each category sought
Correct answer: D. 45 jobs in each category sought
Rule 5E-14.117(7)(a) requires an applicant for Termite/WDO, Lawn and Ornamental, or General Household Pest Control certification to participate in a minimum of 45 jobs in Florida under a certified operator's supervision in each category sought, documented on Form FDACS-13653.
HPC

A ghost ant job involves trails into a bathroom and kitchen, with ants feeding heavily at a honey jar. Which treatment approach fits this species best?

  1. A. A repellent perimeter spray inside and out, repeated monthly, to drive the ants away from all the food and water sources
  2. B. Granular protein bait broadcast across the lawn where the colonies are assumed to be nesting
  3. C. Sweet liquid or gel baits placed along trails and entry points, with no repellent sprays used on the trails
  4. D. Gel bait placed only in the kitchen cabinets, since ghost ant colonies always nest near stored food supplies
Correct answer: C. Sweet liquid or gel baits placed along trails and entry points, with no repellent sprays used on the trails
Ghost ants form multiple-queen colonies that bud readily and nest in wall voids, potted plants, and debris, so killing foragers with repellent sprays just splits and scatters them. Slow-acting sweet baits carried back along their edge-following trails are the standard approach.
Laws & Safety

What does FDACS charge to take the PHPC and Core examinations and to obtain or renew the Public Health Pest Control license?

  1. A. A $100 examination fee is charged for the two examinations, plus a $50 recertification fee
  2. B. An initial certification fee of $75 is charged, although the examinations themselves are free
  3. C. No fees: the examinations, initial certification, and recertification are all free
  4. D. Fees are set by each mosquito control district or county program that employs the licensee
Correct answer: C. No fees: the examinations, initial certification, and recertification are all free
FDACS lists no fees to take the PHPC or Core examinations, no initial certification fee, and no recertification fee for the Public Health Pest Control license (FDACS Mosquito Control Licensing; FDACS CEU Chart, Table 4).
WDO

Under Rule 5E-14.105(2)(h), F.A.C., what must a WDO contract state about retreatments and repairs?

  1. A. That retreatments and repairs are governed by the licensee's standard service policy on file at the business location
  2. B. The conditions under which retreatments for reinfestation and repairs, if any, will be made
  3. C. That the conditions will be set out in an addendum if a reinfestation ever occurs
  4. D. Nothing, unless the contract is sold as a repair contract rather than retreatment only
Correct answer: B. The conditions under which retreatments for reinfestation and repairs, if any, will be made
Per Rule 5E-14.105(2)(h), the contract itself must state the conditions under which retreatments (for reinfestation) and repairs, if any, will be made. Deferring those conditions to a service policy or a later addendum leaves the contract incomplete.
Florida Regulations

Under the degree path in s.482.132, F.S., the applicant's degree must include advanced training or a major in entomology, botany, agronomy, or horticulture. How much coursework in those subjects is required?

  1. A. At least 20 semester hours or 30 quarter hours
  2. B. At least 24 semester hours or 36 quarter hours
  3. C. At least 15 semester hours or 22 quarter hours
  4. D. At least 30 semester hours or 45 quarter hours
Correct answer: A. At least 20 semester hours or 30 quarter hours
Per s.482.132(2)(b), the degree path requires at least 20 semester hours or 30 quarter hours in entomology, botany, agronomy, or horticulture, plus 1 year of employment as a service employee of a licensee in the category sought.
HPC

Calls about Asian cockroaches getting into Florida homes peak in mid-to-late summer, even at accounts with no indoor roach history. The main reason is:

  1. A. Outdoor populations in mulch and ground cover peak then, and large numbers of fliers blunder indoors around lights and doorways
  2. B. Late summer is when the roaches' indoor colonies finish developing in wall voids and begin sending out swarmers
  3. C. Insecticides applied around homes in spring wear off by late summer and release a surge of indoor roaches
  4. D. The roaches migrate indoors in late summer to escape the heavy seasonal rains and flooding in the yard
Correct answer: A. Outdoor populations in mulch and ground cover peak then, and large numbers of fliers blunder indoors around lights and doorways
Asian cockroaches build up outdoors through the warm season, reaching peak numbers in mid-to-late summer in shaded mulch, leaf litter, and ground cover. At peak density their evening flights to lights bring many of them to windows, doors, and porches — and some get inside — even though the species does not live indoors.

WDO

Under s.482.226(2)(a), F.S., which three items identifying the inspection must every WDO inspection report include?

  1. A. The licensee's name, the inspection fee charged, and the address of the structure inspected
  2. B. The inspector's card number, the date of inspection, and the address of the structure inspected
  3. C. The licensee's name, the date of inspection, and the address of the structure inspected
  4. D. The licensee's name, the date of inspection, and the name of the property owner
Correct answer: C. The licensee's name, the date of inspection, and the address of the structure inspected
Per s.482.226(2)(a), the report must include the licensee's name, the date of inspection, and the address of the structure inspected. The fee charged, the inspector's card number, and the owner's name are not among the required items.
Florida Regulations

A worker is paid by a pest control licensee as an independent contractor, with no federal tax withholding or matching by the licensee. Under the s.482.021 definition, is this worker an "employee" of the licensee?

  1. A. Yes — anyone who performs pest control work for a licensee counts as an employee under Chapter 482
  2. B. No — an employee works under the operator in charge and has federal taxes deducted and matched by the licensee
  3. C. Yes — independent contractors are treated as employees once they hold an employee identification card
  4. D. No — a worker is only an employee after completing the required field training in a pest control category
Correct answer: B. No — an employee works under the operator in charge and has federal taxes deducted and matched by the licensee
Per s.482.021(8), an employee is a person employed by a licensee, under the personal supervision and direct control of the licensee's certified operator in charge, from whose compensation the licensee regularly deducts and matches federal insurance contributions and federal income and Social Security taxes. A contractor with no withholding or matching fails that definition.
HPC

A Florida tenant keeps finding ghost ants in a bathroom even after visible trails are cleaned up and sprayed. The colonies are most likely harbored in:

  1. A. A single large mound nest out in the lawn that sends foragers up the exterior wall each night
  2. B. Wall voids and the soil of potted plants near the plumbing, in several small separate nests
  3. C. The drain trap and sink overflow, where one queen maintains a single nest for the whole colony
  4. D. Dry attic insulation above the bathroom, as far as possible from any moisture source in the home
Correct answer: B. Wall voids and the soil of potted plants near the plumbing, in several small separate nests
Ghost ants nest in small, easily moved colonies — in wall voids, under tubs and sinks, behind baseboards, and in potted plant soil — with many queens, so activity reappears from several harborage points at once. Baiting all the active sites near moisture works better than chasing any single trail.

Common questions

What score do I need to pass the Florida certified operator exam?

75%, under Rule 5E-14.123. PestPass grades Florida practice at 75% (Florida mosquito control is the exception at 70%).

How much is the Florida certified operator exam?

The FDACS certified operator exam fee is $300. The fumigation Special ID exam is $200, and Limited Certification exams are $150.

Does a Florida ID cardholder take an exam?

No. Under s.482.091, ID cardholders (Florida's registered technicians) complete training only &mdash; there is no exam. PestPass's Florida ID Cardholder track reflects that, covering general knowledge, household pest control and Florida regulations as training material.
