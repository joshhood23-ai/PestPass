What does the HPC category cover?

Identification and biology of household pests, control methods (baits, residuals, exclusion, sanitation), application safety, and the judgment calls a certified operator makes on real accounts — kitchens, multi-unit housing, sensitive sites. Questions are written at real operator difficulty.

HPC

Which cockroach has two dark parallel stripes on its pronotum?

  1. A. American cockroach
  2. B. German cockroach, with two dark stripes on the pronotum
  3. C. Oriental cockroach
  4. D. Smokybrown cockroach, a large dark species found in trees and mulch
Correct answer: B. German cockroach, with two dark stripes on the pronotum
The German cockroach is 1/2 inch, tan, with two dark stripes on the pronotum. Most common small indoor cockroach in homes.

Why do broadcast sprays counteract gel bait programs?

  1. A. Repellent sprays contaminate bait placements, keeping cockroaches from feeding on them
  2. B. Broadcast sprays are simply too expensive to justify using alongside a gel bait program
  3. C. Broadcast sprays break down and lose residual effectiveness faster than gel bait does
  4. D. Broadcast sprays cause pyrethroid resistance to develop much faster than gel baits used alone
Correct answer: A. Repellent sprays contaminate bait placements, keeping cockroaches from feeding on them
Repellent insecticide sprays contaminate bait placements — cockroaches detect and avoid the spray residue and won't feed on bait near it.

Which flea species causes most infestations?

  1. A. Human flea (Pulex irritans)
  2. B. Dog flea (Ctenocephalides canis)
  3. C. Rat flea (Xenopsylla cheopis)
  4. D. Cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis)
Correct answer: D. Cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis)
The cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis) causes the vast majority of flea infestations — it feeds on cats, dogs, wildlife, and humans.

Primary bed bug harborage sites include:

  1. A. Mattress seams, box spring, headboard, cracks within 5-20 feet of the host
  2. B. Only within stored luggage and neatly folded clothing items in closets
  3. C. Deep within the soil found directly underneath the building's foundation slab
  4. D. Only inside wall voids and directly behind electrical outlet covers
Correct answer: A. Mattress seams, box spring, headboard, cracks within 5-20 feet of the host
Bed bugs prefer tight spaces close to the sleeping host — mattress seams, box spring, bed frame joints, headboard, and cracks in nearby walls and furniture.
HPC

The two-step fire ant IPM method involves:

  1. A. Spraying the entire property first, then following up with a granular product
  2. B. A bait-only program that never includes any individual mound treatment
  3. C. Drenching every individual mound with an emulsifiable concentrate, then monthly monitoring
  4. D. Broadcasting slow-acting bait over the area PLUS treating individual problem mounds
Correct answer: D. Broadcasting slow-acting bait over the area PLUS treating individual problem mounds
Step 1: broadcast slow-acting bait (workers carry it to queens). Step 2: treat individual mounds needing faster knockdown. Achieves 80-90% population reduction.
HPC

The most important step in long-term small fly (drain fly, fruit fly) control is:

  1. A. Installing UV light traps at every building entry point
  2. B. Locating and eliminating the larval breeding source
  3. C. Using pyrethrin aerosol sprays applied twice daily
  4. D. A monthly broadcast insecticide application program
Correct answer: B. Locating and eliminating the larval breeding source
Source elimination is the cornerstone — without removing the breeding site (drain biofilm, overripe fruit, wet organic matter), adult kills only provide temporary relief.
HPC

Fall-invading pests (stink bugs, lady beetles, boxelder bugs) are best managed by:

  1. A. A broadcast interior spray applied throughout September before they enter
  2. B. Placing glue boards scattered randomly throughout the entire structure
  3. C. Exclusion before migration — seal entry points and treat the perimeter
  4. D. Fogger applications carried out repeatedly in October, after they've entered
Correct answer: C. Exclusion before migration — seal entry points and treat the perimeter
Exclusion before entry (late summer/early fall): seal cracks, install door sweeps, repair screens. Vacuum interior populations — avoid crushing stink bugs (odor) and lady beetles (staining).

Before treating a new cockroach account, the FIRST action should be:

  1. A. Conduct a thorough inspection to ID species, severity, and harborage
  2. B. Apply the company's standard treatment program to the account immediately
  3. C. Review which products happen to already be stocked on the truck
  4. D. Simply ask the customer what they personally think the problem is
Correct answer: A. Conduct a thorough inspection to ID species, severity, and harborage
A thorough pre-treatment inspection is required: identify pest species, assess infestation severity, locate harborage, find conducive conditions, and document all findings before selecting treatment.
HPC

A technician identifies pharaoh ants in a hospital. The FIRST treatment action should be:

  1. A. Apply a broadcast spray after hours, once the facility is empty
  2. B. Drill and dust every wall void found throughout the facility
  3. C. Apply a repellent spray immediately to all visible foraging trails
  4. D. Place non-repellent bait along foraging trails and near nests
Correct answer: D. Place non-repellent bait along foraging trails and near nests
Pharaoh ants require non-repellent bait only. Any repellent spray causes colony budding — creating more colonies throughout the facility. Bait is placed along trails and near known nesting areas.
HPC

The MOST effective long-term control for fruit flies in a bar is:

  1. A. Applying a broad space spray treatment on a fixed weekly schedule throughout the entire bar
  2. B. Installing UV light traps positioned near the bar's main entry points and doorway exits
  3. C. Eliminating fermenting material — cleaning drains, removing overripe fruit, soda holsters
  4. D. A thorough residual spray applied to every exposed surface throughout the entire bar area
Correct answer: C. Eliminating fermenting material — cleaning drains, removing overripe fruit, soda holsters
Fruit flies breed in any fermenting organic material — drain sediment, soda gun holsters, forgotten fruit. Source elimination is always primary. UV traps and sprays supplement but cannot solve a sanitation problem.
HPC

American cockroaches are primarily found in:

  1. A. Warm, moist areas such as sewers, steam tunnels, and basements
  2. B. Inside electronic equipment housings and small appliance motors
  3. C. Kitchen food-prep surfaces and the interiors of nearby cabinets
  4. D. Dry, upper wall voids located away from any moisture source
Correct answer: A. Warm, moist areas such as sewers, steam tunnels, and basements
American cockroaches (Periplaneta americana) prefer very warm, moist environments. They are common in sewers, steam tunnels, utility chases, and basements, and typically enter structures through floor drains and sewer connections.
HPC

Which stored product pest is a serious threat specifically to tobacco products, but also readily infests dried herbs and spices, dried pet food, and museum specimens?

  1. A. Indianmeal moth
  2. B. Sawtoothed grain beetle
  3. C. Cadelle beetle
  4. D. Cigarette beetle
Correct answer: D. Cigarette beetle
The cigarette beetle is best known for damaging stored tobacco, but its diet is far broader — dried herbs and spices, dried pet food, and museum or herbarium specimens are all common infestation sources, which is why identifying the beetle doesn't automatically tell you which product to inspect.
HPC

Fabric pests (clothes moths, carpet beetles) primarily damage textiles containing:

  1. A. Keratin, a protein found in wool, fur, feathers, and hair
  2. B. Any fabric at all, regardless of its specific fiber content
  3. C. Pure synthetic fibers like polyester, with no natural fiber blend
  4. D. Cotton exclusively, with no other fiber type present
Correct answer: A. Keratin, a protein found in wool, fur, feathers, and hair
Fabric pest larvae feed on keratin, a protein found in wool, fur, feathers, and hair — not on synthetic fibers. (Silk is fibroin and leather is collagen, not keratin, though larvae may still damage soiled silk and leather.) A pure polyester or nylon item is not a food source unless it is blended with natural fiber or heavily soiled with food or body oils that attract feeding.
HPC

Broadcast fire ant bait works by:

  1. A. Killing workers instantly on contact, before they can return to the mound
  2. B. Being carried back by foraging workers to eventually reach and affect the queen
  3. C. Dissolving the mound structure directly through a chemical reaction in the soil
  4. D. Repelling ants away from the treated area rather than actually killing the colony
Correct answer: B. Being carried back by foraging workers to eventually reach and affect the queen
Broadcast bait relies on foraging worker ants carrying it back to the colony, eventually reaching the queen — this is what reduces or eliminates the colony rather than just killing exposed workers.

How can a smoky brown cockroach be distinguished from an American cockroach at a glance?

  1. A. Smoky brown is uniformly dark with no markings; the American has a pale figure-8 marking on the pronotum
  2. B. The American cockroach is always noticeably smaller in body length than the smoky brown cockroach species
  3. C. Smoky brown cockroaches are always pale white in color, unlike the reddish-brown American cockroach adults
  4. D. The smoky brown has a pale band around the pronotum, while the American cockroach is uniformly dark
Correct answer: A. Smoky brown is uniformly dark with no markings; the American has a pale figure-8 marking on the pronotum
The smoky brown cockroach's uniformly dark mahogany-brown body (no pale marking) distinguishes it from the American cockroach's distinctive pale figure-8 marking on the pronotum, even though both are large and often confused at a glance.
HPC

True or False: only adult bed bugs are capable of being transported into a new location and starting an infestation there.

  1. A. True, but only mated female adults are capable of starting a new infestation
  2. B. False — only unhatched eggs are actually capable of surviving transport intact
  3. C. True — only fully mature adult bed bugs are capable of surviving the transport process
  4. D. False — nymphs and even eggs can be transported in luggage, furniture, or clothing
Correct answer: D. False — nymphs and even eggs can be transported in luggage, furniture, or clothing
Bed bugs at any life stage — eggs, nymphs, or adults — can be inadvertently transported and establish a new infestation, which is why travel-related precautions address more than just adult insects.

Which cockroach species is most strongly associated with sewer systems and is capable of entering structures through plumbing drains?

  1. A. American cockroach
  2. B. German cockroach
  3. C. Brown-banded cockroach
  4. D. Field cockroach
Correct answer: A. American cockroach
American cockroaches are strongly associated with sewers, steam tunnels, and damp underground infrastructure, and are known to travel through plumbing, including emerging from drains — a route German and brown-banded cockroaches don’t typically use.
HPC

Large dark roaches are living in tree holes and mulch beds around a home and flying to the porch light at dusk. Which species fits best, and why does the harborage matter for control?

  1. A. Smokybrown cockroach — outdoor, arboreal; target exterior harborage
  2. B. German cockroach — an indoor species that has recently moved outdoors
  3. C. Oriental cockroach — a sewer species that nests in tree holes
  4. D. American cockroach — which never flies and stays inside sewers
Correct answer: A. Smokybrown cockroach — outdoor, arboreal; target exterior harborage
Smokybrown cockroaches are outdoor, arboreal, strong fliers attracted to light; the population lives in tree holes, mulch, and gutters — not sewers or kitchens. Treating this like an indoor German cockroach problem (cabinet baiting) misses the source entirely; exterior harborage reduction and perimeter treatment are the strategy.

Which statement correctly distinguishes recluse spiders from most other spider species in terms of eye arrangement?

  1. A. Recluse spiders have six eyes in three distinct pairs; most other spiders have eight eyes
  2. B. Recluse spiders are said to have twelve eyes, more than any other spider genus
  3. C. Recluse spiders are believed to lack functional eyes entirely and to rely only on vibration
  4. D. Eye count is identical across all spider species and cannot be used for identification
Correct answer: A. Recluse spiders have six eyes in three distinct pairs; most other spiders have eight eyes
Most spiders have eight eyes, but recluse spiders are among the exceptions with only six, arranged in three distinct pairs — a genuinely useful identification feature when a specimen can be examined closely.

Which statement about drain flies (moth flies) breeding sites is most accurate for structural pest control purposes?

  1. A. Drain flies breed in the gelatinous organic film inside drain pipes, not in the water itself
  2. B. Drain fly larvae feed only on live plant material, never on organic sludge or film
  3. C. Drain flies require direct outdoor soil contact in order to complete their full life cycle successfully
  4. D. Drain flies breed exclusively in clean standing water with no organic material present
Correct answer: A. Drain flies breed in the gelatinous organic film inside drain pipes, not in the water itself
Drain fly larvae develop in the gelatinous organic film that builds up on the inner walls of drain pipes, not in the water passing through — which is why simply running water through a drain doesn’t reliably eliminate an active breeding site.
HPC

Which statement about the Oriental cockroach's flight capability is accurate?

  1. A. Oriental cockroaches are the strongest fliers of all structural cockroach pests
  2. B. Flight capability is essentially identical across every structural cockroach species
  3. C. Oriental cockroaches cannot fly at all, unlike some other structural cockroach species
  4. D. Oriental cockroaches fly readily at dusk, much like smokybrown cockroaches do
Correct answer: C. Oriental cockroaches cannot fly at all, unlike some other structural cockroach species
Oriental cockroaches cannot fly at all — males have only short wings and females mere wing pads, so they run or drop but never take flight, unlike species such as the American cockroach. That detail can factor into exclusion and entry-point assessment during an inspection.

Which ant species is tiny (about 1/16 inch), light yellow to amber in color, and known for splitting into new colonies through a process called budding?

  1. A. Pharaoh ant
  2. B. Fire ant
  3. C. Argentine ant
  4. D. Odorous house ant
Correct answer: A. Pharaoh ant
Pharaoh ants are extremely small, pale yellow ants that reproduce through budding, quickly forming new satellite colonies that make them notoriously hard to control. Scientific name: Monomorium pharaonis.

A restaurant kitchen has heavy German cockroach activity behind the refrigerator and under the dishwasher. What is the best treatment approach?

  1. A. Gel bait in cracks and crevices near harborages, plus an insect growth regulator (IGR)
  2. B. A heavy baseboard spray of a repellent pyrethroid applied throughout the whole kitchen area
  3. C. A single total-release fogger discharged in the center of the kitchen at closing
  4. D. Removing the refrigerator and leaving the kitchen vacant for one full week
Correct answer: A. Gel bait in cracks and crevices near harborages, plus an insect growth regulator (IGR)
German roaches live in tight cracks near food, moisture, and warmth. Gel baits placed into those harborages — plus an IGR to break the reproductive cycle — outperform baseboard sprays, which scatter the population.

What is the correct placement for a mouse snap trap along a wall?

  1. A. Flat against the wall, parallel to it, bait facing the room
  2. B. Out in the center of the room, spaced evenly apart
  3. C. Hanging from the ceiling directly above droppings
  4. D. Perpendicular to the wall, trigger end against the wall
Correct answer: D. Perpendicular to the wall, trigger end against the wall
Mice travel along wall edges using their whiskers. Traps set perpendicular to the wall with the trigger (bait) end touching the wall intercept them on their runway.

Thousands of tiny reddish 'bugs' cover a sunny window in spring. They don't bite. What are they, and what should the homeowner avoid doing?

  1. A. They are a type of bed bug that feeds on blood at night
  2. B. They are disease-carrying vectors that infect humans and pets
  3. C. Harmless plant-feeding mites; vacuum them, don't crush
  4. D. They bore into wood framing like powderpost beetles do
Correct answer: C. Harmless plant-feeding mites; vacuum them, don't crush
Clover mites are tiny reddish mites that invade homes in spring and fall, often around windows. They feed on plants, do not bite — and should be vacuumed, not smashed, because they leave red stains.

Three weeks after a flea treatment (adulticide plus IGR), a few adult fleas are still seen but the pet has no new bites and trap counts are falling. What is the correct decision?

  1. A. Retreat immediately — any adult fleas at 3 weeks prove the treatment failed
  2. B. Keep monitoring — pupae still emerging; re-evaluate at 4–6 weeks
  3. C. Declare the job complete and cancel all remaining follow-ups
  4. D. Switch to a different adulticide class applied at double the label rate
Correct answer: B. Keep monitoring — pupae still emerging; re-evaluate at 4–6 weeks
Falling trap counts with no new pet bites indicate a declining population; the few adults are expected emergence from pre-existing pupae, which the IGR prevents from replacing. Retreating on schedule rather than on evidence wastes product and resets the residual clock — the retreatment trigger is a rebound after 4–6 weeks, not lingering emergence.

Which diseases and parasites can the cat flea transmit?

  1. A. Lyme disease and Rocky Mountain spotted fever
  2. B. Plague, murine typhus and tapeworms
  3. C. Hantavirus and rat-bite fever
  4. D. Malaria and dengue
Correct answer: B. Plague, murine typhus and tapeworms
The cat flea feeds on cats, dogs, opossums, raccoons, foxes and humans, and causes most flea infestations technicians see. Lyme disease and Rocky Mountain spotted fever come from ticks.

The label rate is 0.5 fl oz of concentrate per gallon of water. How much concentrate goes into a 3-gallon sprayer?

  1. A. 3 fl oz
  2. B. 0.5 fl oz
  3. C. 1 fl oz
  4. D. 1.5 fl oz
Correct answer: D. 1.5 fl oz
Step 1: Multiply the per-gallon rate by the number of gallons: 0.5 fl oz × 3 gallons = 1.5 fl oz.
HPC

A crack-and-crevice label states 20 fl oz of finished spray per 100 linear feet. Baseboards total 60 linear feet. How much spray is needed?

  1. A. 120 fl oz
  2. B. 6 fl oz
  3. C. 12 fl oz
  4. D. 24 fl oz
Correct answer: C. 12 fl oz
Step 1: Find what fraction of the rate length you have: 60 ft ÷ 100 ft = 0.6. Step 2: Take that fraction of the rate amount: 0.6 × 20 fl oz = 12 fl oz.
HPC

Interior residual sprays usually fail against occasional invaders because:

  1. A. These pests are immune to all insecticides
  2. B. The source population is outdoors — they wander in
  3. C. Interior sprays attract more of them
  4. D. They only invade during winter when sprays freeze
Correct answer: B. The source population is outdoors — they wander in
Millipedes, stink bugs, and lady beetles don't breed indoors; they're incidental invaders from outside. Spraying baseboards treats the symptom. Sealing entry points and treating the exterior perimeter in late summer addresses the source.
