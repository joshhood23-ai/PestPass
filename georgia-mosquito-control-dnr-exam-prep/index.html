Common questions

What score passes the Georgia DNR nuisance wildlife exam?

80% &mdash; higher than Georgia's other pest control exams. PestPass grades DNR practice at the same 80% mark.

Does Florida have an equivalent of the Georgia DNR permit?

No. Florida does not license nuisance wildlife trappers through an equivalent exam; Florida's only exam-based wildlife credential is FDACS's narrow Limited Certification for nonchemical trapping of commensal rodents. The DNR track in PestPass is Georgia-only.

What mosquito categories does PestPass cover?

Mosquito Biology, Larvicides, Adulticides & ULV application, and Regulations & Permits &mdash; plus a General Standards (Core) category shared with the other public-health tracks.
