General Knowledge

According to IPM Feature 1 (Identification), why is proper pest identification essential, and what tools aid it?

  1. A. Identification is only needed for billing records and customer reports; the main tool is a smartphone camera used to document damage, and there is no need to consult published guides, the county extension service, or any insect pest collection.
  2. B. Proper identification gives the PMP access to the body of knowledge on pest biology and life cycle; tools include published guides, the county extension service, and a company insect pest collection for matching specimens.
  3. C. Identification satisfies the label's legal requirement; the only approved tool is a hand lens issued by the state department.
  4. D. Identification helps the customer trust the technician; tools include advertising brochures and the company's social media pages.
Correct answer: B. Proper identification gives the PMP access to the body of knowledge on pest biology and life cycle; tools include published guides, the county extension service, and a company insect pest collection for matching specimens.
IPM Feature 1 is Identification — proper ID gives the PMP access to the body of knowledge on pest biology and life cycle, using published guides, the county extension service, and a company insect pest collection for matching specimens.

Florida Regulations

Before applying for General Household Pest Control certification, an applicant must participate in a minimum number of jobs in Florida under the supervision of a certified operator in each category sought, documented on Form FDACS-13653. Under Rule 5E-14.117, that minimum is:

  1. A. 25 jobs in each category sought
  2. B. 30 jobs in each category sought
  3. C. 60 jobs in each category sought
  4. D. 45 jobs in each category sought
Correct answer: D. 45 jobs in each category sought
Rule 5E-14.117(7)(a) requires an applicant for Termite/WDO, Lawn and Ornamental, or General Household Pest Control certification to participate in a minimum of 45 jobs in Florida under a certified operator's supervision in each category sought, documented on Form FDACS-13653.
HPC

A ghost ant job involves trails into a bathroom and kitchen, with ants feeding heavily at a honey jar. Which treatment approach fits this species best?

  1. A. A repellent perimeter spray inside and out, repeated monthly, to drive the ants away from all the food and water sources
  2. B. Granular protein bait broadcast across the lawn where the colonies are assumed to be nesting
  3. C. Sweet liquid or gel baits placed along trails and entry points, with no repellent sprays used on the trails
  4. D. Gel bait placed only in the kitchen cabinets, since ghost ant colonies always nest near stored food supplies
Correct answer: C. Sweet liquid or gel baits placed along trails and entry points, with no repellent sprays used on the trails
Ghost ants form multiple-queen colonies that bud readily and nest in wall voids, potted plants, and debris, so killing foragers with repellent sprays just splits and scatters them. Slow-acting sweet baits carried back along their edge-following trails are the standard approach.

Which fertility practice is correct for centipedegrass?

  1. A. Target soil pH 5.0–6.0, apply phosphorus only when a soil test calls for it, and do not overseed ryegrass into it
  2. B. Target soil pH 6.5–7.0 and apply phosphorus routinely every spring
  3. C. Overseed ryegrass each fall to protect the centipede crowns from cold
  4. D. Apply nitrogen at bermudagrass rates of 4–5 pounds per 1,000 square feet yearly, regardless of weather conditions
Correct answer: A. Target soil pH 5.0–6.0, apply phosphorus only when a soil test calls for it, and do not overseed ryegrass into it
Centipedegrass fertility cautions: target soil pH 5.0–6.0, skip phosphorus unless a soil test calls for it, and do NOT overseed ryegrass on centipedegrass.

How are chinch bugs sampled in turf?

  1. A. Cutting a 1-square-foot flap of turf and folding it back to count insects in the soil
  2. B. Pouring a soap solution over the turf to drive insects to the surface
  3. C. Under all conditions, sweeping a net across the turf surface at dusk to catch feeding insects, no matter the circumstances
  4. D. Pushing an open-ended can 2–3 inches into the turf, filling it with water, and counting the bugs that float to the surface
Correct answer: D. Pushing an open-ended can 2–3 inches into the turf, filling it with water, and counting the bugs that float to the surface
Chinch bugs are sampled by flotation: an open-ended can is pushed 2–3 inches into the turf, filled with water, and the bugs that float to the surface are counted.
Fumigation

Methyl bromide is still legally permitted under a Critical Use Exemption primarily for:

  1. A. Pre-plant soil fumigation for crops such as strawberries and tomatoes where no feasible alternatives exist
  2. B. Quarantine and pre-shipment treatments at ports of entry, preventing invasive pests on imported goods
  3. C. Subterranean termite soil treatments performed around structures
  4. D. Drywood termite fumigation performed across all fifty states
Correct answer: A. Pre-plant soil fumigation for crops such as strawberries and tomatoes where no feasible alternatives exist
Methyl bromide was phased out under the Montreal Protocol. Critical Use Exemptions covered specific pre-plant soil uses (e.g., strawberries, tomatoes, peppers) with no technically or economically feasible alternatives. Quarantine and pre-shipment (QPS) was a separate standing exemption that never required a CUE, and structural work largely moved to sulfuryl fluoride.
Fumigation

What respiratory protection is required to re-enter a structure before clearance is confirmed?

  1. A. A simple dust mask for airborne particulates
  2. B. Supplied-air or SCBA respiratory protection only
  3. C. A cartridge half-face respirator mask with filters
  4. D. Holding one’s breath and working very quickly inside
Correct answer: B. Supplied-air or SCBA respiratory protection only
Entry before clearance requires supplied-air or SCBA respiratory protection — air-purifying respirators (cartridge masks) do not protect against fumigant gases. This is a non-negotiable safety rule.
Adulticides & ULV

Before starting a neighborhood adulticide route, the applicator should check:

  1. A. Label rate, weather, and notification rules
  2. B. The truck's maintenance schedule for the month
  3. C. Whether residents requested extra-strong spraying
  4. D. That the tank is filled to maximum capacity
Correct answer: A. Label rate, weather, and notification rules
Pre-route checklist: confirm the labeled rate, verify wind and weather are within label limits, and complete any required public notifications. Customer requests for stronger spraying never override the label.
Larvicides

For a large flooded woodland pool full of early-instar larvae, the most practical larvicide is usually:

  1. A. Briquets carefully hand-placed one by one
  2. B. A surface film carefully applied by hand
  3. C. Liquid or granular Bti via ground or air
  4. D. A truck-mounted adulticide fog at dusk
Correct answer: C. Liquid or granular Bti via ground or air
Large, temporary habitats with young larvae are ideal for broadcast Bti — fast, effective, and selective.
