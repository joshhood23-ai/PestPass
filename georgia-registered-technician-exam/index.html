Registered technician vs. certified operator

The registered technician exam covers the fundamentals a working technician needs from day one. The certified operator exam goes deeper and carries legal responsibility for the work. PestPass keeps the two tracks separate — the technician track never serves operator-only material, and vice versa.

General Knowledge

Pesticide resistance is best described as:

  1. A. Genetic survival and reproduction of pests exposed to a pesticide — offspring inherit the trait
  2. B. Pest populations developing a learned behavioral avoidance of treated surfaces over time
  3. C. Applicator sensitivity developing gradually after repeated personal chemical exposure over years
  4. D. Pesticide product simply losing potency due to improper storage conditions over time
Correct answer: A. Genetic survival and reproduction of pests exposed to a pesticide — offspring inherit the trait
Resistance is genetic — individual pests that survive pesticide exposure reproduce, passing resistance traits to offspring. It builds over successive generations of selection pressure.
HPC

Pheromone traps for stored product pests are primarily used for:

  1. A. Monitoring to determine infestation presence, location, size, and trend
  2. B. Attracting and eliminating the entire pest population through the trap alone
  3. C. Killing adult insects directly, serving as the primary control method
  4. D. Preventing any future re-infestation once treatment has been completed
Correct answer: A. Monitoring to determine infestation presence, location, size, and trend
Pheromone traps are monitoring tools, not control tools. They identify which pests are present, where infestations are concentrated, and whether control measures are working. They alone are not population reduction tools for stored product pests.
General Knowledge

A manufacturer voluntarily withdraws a product mid-registration due to newly identified safety concerns. What generally happens to existing stock already purchased by applicators?

  1. A. Existing-stock use follows EPA's cancellation order, which may allow limited use
  2. B. No regulatory guidance of any kind applies to stock that’s already been purchased
  3. C. Existing stock becomes immediately illegal to possess in any quantity at all
  4. D. Existing stock automatically transfers over to a different, unrelated registration
Correct answer: A. Existing-stock use follows EPA's cancellation order, which may allow limited use
EPA cancellation and phase-out orders specify what happens to existing stock — sometimes allowing continued use under existing label terms for a defined period, sometimes requiring specific disposal, depending on the reason for cancellation.
HPC

The drugstore beetle, cigarette beetle and khapra beetle are classed as stored-product:

  1. A. Internal feeders
  2. B. External feeders
  3. C. Scavengers
  4. D. Secondary pests
Correct answer: B. External feeders
External feeders develop outside whole grains and can feed on both finished and unfinished food products such as flour or cereal.
General Knowledge

Which measuring and mixing practice best reduces inhalation exposure when handling a wettable powder?

  1. A. Pouring the powder from shoulder height for a smooth, fast flow
  2. B. Measuring outdoors upwind so dust blows toward the mixer
  3. C. Open the bag and pour below face level in a ventilated area
  4. D. Mixing the powder dry into the empty tank before adding any water
Correct answer: C. Open the bag and pour below face level in a ventilated area
Keep the dust source below the breathing zone, stay upwind of it, ventilate, and avoid agitating the bag — simple work-practice controls that cut airborne dust at the highest-exposure task. Respirator selection still follows the label.
HPC

Which ant species is reddish-brown, builds visible earthen mounds, and delivers a painful sting from a large, aggressive colony?

  1. A. odorous house ant worker
  2. B. Carpenter ant
  3. C. Pavement ant
  4. D. red imported fire ant
Correct answer: D. red imported fire ant
Fire ants are reddish-brown mound-builders known for aggressive defense of the colony and a painful, burning sting. Scientific name: Solenopsis invicta.
General Knowledge

A label lists 'Other ingredients: 97.5%.' A customer asks exactly what those ingredients are. The accurate answer is:

  1. A. The label need not name them; 'other' isn't non-toxic — see the SDS
  2. B. They must be listed by name on the company website for customer review
  3. C. They are always food-grade and completely harmless to people
  4. D. They are all plain water — otherwise the label would say so
Correct answer: A. The label need not name them; 'other' isn't non-toxic — see the SDS
Inert/other ingredients must total 100% with the AI but need not be individually named — some are trade secrets. 'Inert' means non-pesticidal, not non-toxic, so never reassure a customer they are harmless.
HPC

Before treating a new cockroach account, the FIRST action should be:

  1. A. Conduct a thorough inspection to ID species, severity, and harborage
  2. B. Apply the company's standard treatment program to the account immediately
  3. C. Review which products happen to already be stocked on the truck
  4. D. Simply ask the customer what they personally think the problem is
Correct answer: A. Conduct a thorough inspection to ID species, severity, and harborage
A thorough pre-treatment inspection is required: identify pest species, assess infestation severity, locate harborage, find conducive conditions, and document all findings before selecting treatment.
General Knowledge

Structure-infesting rodents (Norway rat, roof rat, house mouse) share which trait?

  1. A. Excellent eyesight and strong color vision, which helps them spot traps from far away
  2. B. Poor eyesight with only limited (dichromatic) color vision, but excellent hearing, taste and smell
  3. C. A lifespan of five years or more, much longer than most people assume for rodents
  4. D. They never gnaw on hard materials like concrete, since their teeth are too soft for it
Correct answer: B. Poor eyesight with only limited (dichromatic) color vision, but excellent hearing, taste and smell
All three live about a year. A rat eats roughly 20 to 40 pounds of food a year but contaminates far more with urine, feces and hair, and their gnawing can damage wiring and start fires.
HPC

What is the key biological difference between millipedes and centipedes?

  1. A. Millipedes are predators of small invertebrates, while centipedes feed on decaying wood
  2. B. Centipedes are simply unable to survive at all in outdoor environments
  3. C. Millipedes actually have noticeably fewer legs than centipedes overall
  4. D. Millipedes feed on decaying plant matter; centipedes prey on small invertebrates
Correct answer: D. Millipedes feed on decaying plant matter; centipedes prey on small invertebrates
Millipedes are detritivores feeding on decaying plant matter, while centipedes are active predators. Ironically, a centipede population indoors can indicate another pest issue is feeding them.

Common questions

What happens if I fail the Georgia registered technician exam twice?

For Registration (the CRE exam), a second failure means waiting 30 calendar days before testing again, under Rule 620-3-.01 as amended in 2026.

Do I need experience before taking the technician exam?

Registration is the entry-level credential for Georgia pest control employees working under a certified operator. Check the Georgia Structural Pest Control Commission's current requirements for the exact eligibility rules before you apply.

Is the technician exam the same as the operator exam?

No. The technician exam covers general knowledge and household pest control fundamentals. The certified operator exam adds deeper categories such as wood-destroying organisms and Georgia regulations, and carries a stricter retake penalty.
