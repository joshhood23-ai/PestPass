Why fumigation sits in the Florida tracks only

Georgia does not license structural fumigation as its own credential, so PestPass serves fumigation material only in Florida mode — a Georgia user will never be served a fumigation question. In Florida, fumigation carries its own exam, its own insurance minimums ($1M/$2M) and its own record rules.

Fumigation

Methyl bromide is still legally permitted under a Critical Use Exemption primarily for:

  1. A. Pre-plant soil fumigation for crops such as strawberries and tomatoes where no feasible alternatives exist
  2. B. Quarantine and pre-shipment treatments at ports of entry, preventing invasive pests on imported goods
  3. C. Subterranean termite soil treatments performed around structures
  4. D. Drywood termite fumigation performed across all fifty states
Correct answer: A. Pre-plant soil fumigation for crops such as strawberries and tomatoes where no feasible alternatives exist
Methyl bromide was phased out under the Montreal Protocol. Critical Use Exemptions covered specific pre-plant soil uses (e.g., strawberries, tomatoes, peppers) with no technically or economically feasible alternatives. Quarantine and pre-shipment (QPS) was a separate standing exemption that never required a CUE, and structural work largely moved to sulfuryl fluoride.

What respiratory protection is required to re-enter a structure before clearance is confirmed?

  1. A. A simple dust mask for airborne particulates
  2. B. Supplied-air or SCBA respiratory protection only
  3. C. A cartridge half-face respirator mask with filters
  4. D. Holding one’s breath and working very quickly inside
Correct answer: B. Supplied-air or SCBA respiratory protection only
Entry before clearance requires supplied-air or SCBA respiratory protection — air-purifying respirators (cartridge masks) do not protect against fumigant gases. This is a non-negotiable safety rule.
Fumigation

Sulfuryl fluoride is classified as:

  1. A. A fumigant — a gas that kills pests in enclosed spaces
  2. B. A restricted use herbicide intended for weed control
  3. C. A systemic insecticide that is absorbed by plant tissue
  4. D. A contact insecticide applied directly to surfaces
Correct answer: A. A fumigant — a gas that kills pests in enclosed spaces
Sulfuryl fluoride is a fumigant — a gas that penetrates wood, furniture, and enclosed spaces to kill pests at all life stages. It must be used by certified fumigators.

A label calls for 3 lbs of fumigant per 1,000 cubic feet. A structure measures 20,000 cubic feet. How much fumigant is required?

  1. A. 30 lbs
  2. B. 45 lbs
  3. C. 3 lbs
  4. D. 60 lbs
Correct answer: D. 60 lbs
Step 1: 20,000 ÷ 1,000 = 20 units of volume. Step 2: 20 × 3 lbs = 60 lbs.

What is the minimum number of gas detector readings required to clear a fumigated structure?

  1. A. A single reading taken right at the front door of the structure
  2. B. One reading taken in each individual room of the structure
  3. C. Multiple readings — high and low points throughout each area
  4. D. One reading taken at the center of the largest room in the house
Correct answer: C. Multiple readings — high and low points throughout each area
Clearance requires multiple readings throughout the structure — at both high and low points in each area, since gas concentration can vary by location. A single reading is insufficient for proper clearance.

A structure is fully tarped and sealed for fumigation. A crew member assumes it is now completely inaccessible for the full fumigation period. What is the risk in that assumption?

  1. A. No risk — sealed tarps stay fully airtight the whole period
  2. B. Full tarping eliminates the need for any posted exterior warning signage around the structure
  3. C. The assumption is correct — no monitoring is needed once tarped
  4. D. Seals can gap over time, so continuous monitoring is still required
Correct answer: D. Seals can gap over time, so continuous monitoring is still required
A tarp seal that was sound at setup can develop gaps from wind, wear, or ground movement — that’s why continuous monitoring throughout the fumigation period is required, not just a one-time check at sealing.

Which is the most reliable method to confirm a fumigated structure is actually safe for re-entry?

  1. A. A quick visual check confirming all tarps have been removed
  2. B. Gas monitoring with calibrated equipment at multiple points
  3. C. Waiting the labeled aeration time with no further verification at all
  4. D. Simply asking occupants if they notice any unusual lingering odor
Correct answer: B. Gas monitoring with calibrated equipment at multiple points
Only direct, calibrated gas concentration monitoring at multiple points reliably confirms safe re-entry — elapsed time, visual checks, and human smell (especially for odorless fumigants) are not adequate substitutes.
Fumigation

You are preparing a grain bin for phosphine fumigation with aluminum phosphide tablets. Which preparation step is required because of phosphine's chemical properties?

  1. A. Remove or protect copper wiring and electronics — phosphine corrodes copper
  2. B. Fill the bin to maximum capacity, since phosphine only works in full bins
  3. C. Wet down the grain surface, since phosphine needs high moisture to release
  4. D. Leave aeration fans running to keep diluting phosphine below its odor threshold
Correct answer: A. Remove or protect copper wiring and electronics — phosphine corrodes copper
Phosphine is corrosive to copper and copper alloys (and can damage electronic equipment), so motors, wiring, and electronics in the fumigated space must be removed, disconnected, or protected. Phosphine is released by moisture reacting with the metal phosphide formulation, but wetting grain is not a preparation step; and running aeration fans during exposure would vent the fumigant and defeat the treatment.

Why are fumigation logs treated as legal-compliance documents rather than ordinary business paperwork?

  1. A. They are only used to calculate the customer's final bill for the fumigation job
  2. B. They replace the need for a written fumigation contract entirely
  3. C. They are optional records kept only for marketing purposes by the firm
  4. D. They prove label compliance if a poisoning or complaint is investigated
Correct answer: D. They prove label compliance if a poisoning or complaint is investigated
The log - fumigant type and lot, cubic footage, dosage applied, introduction and exposure times, temperature, clearance readings, personnel and license numbers, re-entry authorization - is the evidence that the job was performed per the label and state rules. In a poisoning investigation or lawsuit, it is the document that shows (or fails to show) legal compliance. Billing details belong in business records, not the fumigation log.

Which statement most accurately describes why some fumigation companies use dual-gas detection instruments during a job?

  1. A. Dual instruments are used purely for redundancy in case one loses battery power
  2. B. A second instrument is only used if the first is a different color
  3. C. Regulations require exactly two readings no matter the instrument type
  4. D. Instruments cross-check each other to catch interference or calibration drift
Correct answer: D. Instruments cross-check each other to catch interference or calibration drift
Using multiple detection methods or cross-checking instruments helps guard against a single device’s calibration drift or environmental interference giving a misleading reading — added confidence in a measurement that clearance decisions directly depend on.

Common questions

How much is the Florida fumigation Special ID exam?

$200. Renewal is $100, with a $25 late fee. (The underlying rule text lists a different figure, but $200 is what FDACS actually charges &mdash; PestPass keys the real-world fee.)

What score passes the Florida fumigation exam?

75%, the standard Florida certified operator pass mark under Rule 5E-14.123.

How long must fumigation records be kept in Florida?

Two years for general and fumigation records. New-construction pretreatment records are kept three years.
