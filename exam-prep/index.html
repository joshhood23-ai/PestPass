Good to know

Questions

Is PestPass free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up or account. Open it in your browser and start studying.

Which exams does it cover?

Georgia: Certified Operator, Registered Technician, DNR nuisance wildlife, Lawn & Ornamental Category 24 and mosquito control. Florida: Certified Operator categories, Fumigation Special ID, Limited Certification, ID Cardholder and Chapter 388 mosquito control.

Can I study on my phone?

Yes. PestPass runs in your phone's browser and installs to your home screen like an app. It runs online, so what you study is always current.

Is PestPass affiliated with the state?

No. PestPass is an independent study tool, not affiliated with the Georgia Department of Agriculture, the GSPCC, FDACS or any testing provider.
