About PestPass

PestPass is a free study app for the pest control licensing exams — built by a working pest control technician who needed a better way to study for his own Certified Operator exam, and couldn't find one.

Why it exists

The official study material for these exams is thorough — and dry. Plenty of technicians learn better by drilling questions than by re-reading a manual, but there was no free, phone-friendly way to do that for the Georgia and Florida structural pest control exams. PestPass was built to fill that gap: a study tool its maker uses himself, on job sites and off, that works with no signal and no sign-up.

What's inside

Georgia: Certified Operator, Registered Technician, Lawn & Ornamental (Category 24), Mosquito Control and the DNR nuisance wildlife permit.

Florida: Certified Operator under Chapter 482, Fumigation (Special ID), Lawn & Ornamental, Limited Certification and Mosquito Control under Chapter 388.

Every track draws only its own exam's questions, written to the same rules, categories and difficulty as the real test, each with an explanation. Alongside the questions there are flashcards, photo ID drills, timed games and a missed-question review, so weak spots get extra work automatically.

How it works

Open pestpass.app in any browser and start studying — no account, no download, no cost. Once it has loaded, the questions and flashcards work fully offline (ads, when shown, need a connection). Progress is stored on your own device, separately for each state and exam track.
